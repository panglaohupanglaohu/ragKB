# Research Deliberation: Plaza-to-Evolution Closed Loop

## Knowledge Consolidation

1. **Plaza**: Multi-agent LLM discussion generates TaskHabitatContracts. Structured methods (ORID, Six Hats, Fist-to-Five) improve discussion quality over unstructured Moderator-led approaches.
2. **Evolution (EcoDrill)**: Natural selection via metabolic constraint (Health Ledger + ecological pressure parameters A,P,D,K) selects agents with better skill-niche matching. 9-run data: collab% inverted-U (R²=0.43, p<0.01), dominant skills emerge from 9-gen mixed competition.
3. **Skill assignment**: Dominant skills from evolution can be written back to agent profiles.
4. **Closed loop**: Discuss → Extract skills → Evolve under pressure → Validate with τ → Assign dominant skills → Re-discuss.

## Selected Hypotheses

**H1** (Plaza structure downstream effect): ORID-structured Plaza produces contracts with higher niche-skill specificity. Tested by comparing contract specificity scores between ORID and baseline Plaza modes.

**H2** (Evolution as optimizer): Multi-generation mixed competition under moderate ecological pressure (A≈0.55) produces dominant skills that, when re-assigned to teams, improve mean survival τ by ≥15%.

**H3** (Closed-loop convergence): 3+ iterations of Discuss→Extract→Evolve→Validate→Re-equip converges to a stable dominant skill set.

## Pre-specified Success Criteria
1. ORID contracts show higher niche-skill matching specificity
2. Dominant-skill teams show higher mean τ than random-skill teams
3. Skill set stabilizes within 3 iterations
